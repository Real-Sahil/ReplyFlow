Replies, orders and the books, in one place.

ReplyFlow is for owner-run UK shops and makers who sell through Facebook and Messenger. Answer customers, follow orders, send invoices, see who owes you and when money gets tight, and let staff send expenses and hours from their phones.

Book a demo See what it does

Know who owes you, today

Every invoice you have issued and not been paid for sits on one list, with the days late and a reminder message you can copy in one tap. Record the payment on the order and it drops off.

Bills you owe suppliers sit underneath, so you see both sides of the same page.

Screens use made-up shop data.

See when money may be tight

Type in what is in the bank. ReplyFlow adds what customers owe, the bills coming due and the payments you have planned, and shows the next 30, 60 or 90 days. If the balance would dip below zero, it names the day.

It is a guide, not a promise, and it says so on the page.

Screens use made-up shop data.

Staff spend money. You see it all.

Each person gets their own sign-in, made by you in person, with no email invitation to lose. They send expense claims with a photo of the receipt, record their hours and ask for holiday. You approve or refuse, and you are told as it arrives.

The same receipt sent twice, or the same amount sent again within three days, is flagged before you pay it.

Screens use made-up shop data.

And the rest of running the place

Contacts, deals and orders. A Messenger inbox and Facebook posting. Numbered invoices and credit notes with VAT. A money book, bills, mileage and bank matching. Repeating rent and subscriptions. A read-only login for your accountant. The full list is on the features page.

What it is not

It does not run payroll and it does not replace your accountant. It keeps good records, adds up hours and holiday, and gives your accountant clean numbers and their own read-only login. It follows UK VAT. Other markets and currencies are supported for amounts and invoices, without local tax rules yet.
