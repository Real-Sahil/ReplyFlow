Staff expenses without the carrier bag of receipts

5 November 2026 · The ReplyFlow team

Somewhere in your business there's a carrier bag of receipts. It might be a drawer, an envelope or a photo roll. It gets sorted once a month, in a hurry, and something always goes missing.

The cost isn't the lost receipt. It's the argument three weeks later about whether you've already paid it.

Claim at the moment of spending

The fix is boring: make it easier to claim straight away than to claim later. A phone, a photo of the receipt, an amount, a category. Thirty seconds, while the person is still standing in the car park.

That means each person needs their own way in. We give each staff member a sign-in the administrator creates in person, with a one-time password. No invitation email to go astray, and they only see their own claims.

Catch the repeat before you pay it

Honest people claim twice. They forget they sent it, or they send the photo and the screenshot. So the app compares each new claim with the old ones. The same receipt photo, or the same person claiming the same amount within three days, gets a flag. A flag isn't a block. You see it, you decide, and the button says Approve anyway so you know what you're doing.

Know when it arrives

You get a notification when a claim comes in, and a batch of claims is one notification, not forty. Approve or refuse with a reason the person can read, then mark it paid back when the money has gone.

What it won't do

It doesn't decide what's a legitimate business expense. That's your call and your accountant's. It makes sure you're deciding with the receipt in front of you.
