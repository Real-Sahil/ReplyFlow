# Privacy policy (published at /privacy on the website)

The published page is generated from `scripts/marketing/build.py` (the `privacy` text). This file is a plain
copy for the Meta app review pack. Keep the two the same.

**Who this covers.** ReplyFlow is software that organisations use to answer customers, follow orders and keep
their records. This policy explains how personal information is handled when you deal with an organisation that
uses ReplyFlow, and when you use ReplyFlow's own website or sign in to the app. The organisation you deal with
decides why and how your information is used and is responsible for it. ReplyFlow processes it on the
organisation's behalf, only for the purposes the organisation sets.

**What may be collected.**
- Details given in an enquiry or booking: name, email address, phone number and what you are interested in.
- Messages and comments sent to an organisation's Facebook Page, and messages sent through Messenger.
- Answers given on a Facebook lead form.
- Order details: what was bought, delivery details, payments and updates sent about the order.
- For people who work for an organisation and sign in: name, work email, role, and the expense claims, hours and
  holiday requests they send.
- Basic technical information needed to keep the service secure and working.

**Why it is used.** To answer enquiries, arrange and deliver orders, keep people updated, handle after-sales
issues, pay and record staff expenses, keep the financial records an organisation must keep, and keep the
service secure. Information is not sold and is not used to build advertising profiles.

**How long it is kept.** Enquiry and messaging records are kept while the organisation is in touch with you and
removed when you ask the organisation to remove them. Order, invoice and payment records are kept for as long as
the law requires. Records are separated by organisation.

**Who it is shared with.** Only the services needed to run the product, acting on instructions: Supabase
(database and file hosting), Vercel (website and app hosting), Meta (Facebook and Messenger), Postmark (email),
and an AI writing service that is sent product details, not customer details, to draft posts. Information may
also be shared when the law requires it.

**Your rights.** You can ask to see, correct or delete the information held about you, and to object to or
restrict how it is used. Ask the organisation that holds it. To delete data you sent through Facebook or
Messenger, send a deletion request from Facebook's data settings. For ReplyFlow's own website and accounts, use
the contact page on the website.

**Cookies and storage.** No advertising or tracking cookies. The app stores a sign-in session and a few
preferences on your device.

**Changes.** Updated when practices change. Last updated: 5 November 2026.
