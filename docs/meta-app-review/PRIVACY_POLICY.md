# Privacy policy: Mere & Row (draft for publishing on the website)

**Who we are.** Mere & Row is a sofa business. Our address is [COMPANY ADDRESS]. You can
contact us at [PRIVACY EMAIL]. [ICO REGISTRATION NUMBER, if registered].

**What we collect.**
- Details you give us when you enquire: name, email address, phone number and what you are
  interested in.
- Messages and comments you send to our Facebook Page, and messages you send through
  Messenger.
- Answers you give on a Facebook lead form.

**Why we use it.** To answer your enquiry, arrange and deliver your order, keep you updated,
and handle any after-sales issue. We also use it to keep our records for accounting.

and removed on request. Order and payment records are kept for the period the law requires.

**Who we share it with.** Our service providers: Supabase (database hosting), Vercel (website
hosting), Meta (Facebook and Messenger), and Postmark (email). We do not sell your data.
